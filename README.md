# civgrad — the civilization gradient

[![License: Apache-2.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/code-Apache--2.0-blue.svg)](LICENSE)
[![Docs: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/docs-CC%20BY%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](LICENSE-DOCS)
[![validate](https://github.com/a20r/civgrad/actions/workflows/validate.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/a20r/civgrad/actions/workflows/validate.yml)

A differentiable, hierarchical Petri net of the global supply chain. Goal:
locate where marginal investment buys the most marginal resilience — and make
the map improvable by the people who actually know each territory.

The model is three layers over one net (currently: a semiconductor slice with
oracles at every frontier):

- **`core/net.py`** — discrete Petri net: places are stocks, transitions are
  processes, read arcs are capital equipment, inhibitor arcs are policy.
  Finds structural SPOFs, deadlocks, and doomed states (the fab⇄equipment
  livelock: material flows forever, no chip can ever be made again).
- **`core/continuous.py` + `core/gradients.py`** — JAX fluid relaxation.
  Differentiates total chip throughput w.r.t. every capacity and stockpile:
  the ranked gradient vector is *marginal resilience per marginal dollar*,
  averaged over a disruption prior so no single catastrophe drives it.
- **`core/adaptation.py`** — capacity responds to scarcity (runway + unfilled
  demand); recovery time is emergent, with the single gain ALPHA fitted on
  one training event and frozen.

What keeps it honest: historical replays with frozen parameters
(`validation/`), a public scorecard that shows misses as prominently as hits,
and provenance (`{value, units, source, confidence, date}`) on every
parameter in the map (`map/`).

## Scorecard

Auto-generated by `python3 -m validation.runner` — [SCORECARD.md](SCORECARD.md)
is the source of truth; the current table is inlined here.

| event | protocol | model dip | model recovery | historical record | status |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| 2022 neon shock (Russia-Ukraine war: Ingas + Cryoin halt) | v0 imposed-τ | 0.1% | 15.8 mo | no fab stoppages; supply normalized ~12-18mo | ✅ pass |
| 2022 neon shock (Russia-Ukraine war: Ingas + Cryoin halt) | v1 adaptive | -0.7% | 0.0 mo | no fab stoppages; supply normalized ~12-18mo | ✅ pass |
| 2011 Tohoku earthquake (advanced wafer supply) | v0 imposed-τ | -1.7% | 0.0 mo | minor global impact | ✅ pass |
| 2011 Tohoku earthquake (advanced wafer supply) | v1 adaptive | -0.6% | 0.0 mo | minor global impact | ✅ pass |
| 1993 Sumitomo Chemical epoxy-resin plant explosion (chip packaging) | v0 imposed-τ | -1.7% | 0.0 mo | price spike, brief pain, no catastrophe | ❌ xfail (documented miss) |
| 1993 Sumitomo Chemical epoxy-resin plant explosion (chip packaging) | v1 adaptive | 57.6% | never (72 mo horizon) | price spike, brief pain, no catastrophe | ❌ xfail (documented miss) |
| 2019 Japan-Korea photoresist export curbs (analog replay) | v0 imposed-τ | -1.9% | 0.0 mo | non-event | ✅ pass |
| 2019 Japan-Korea photoresist export curbs (analog replay) | v1 adaptive | -0.7% | 0.0 mo | non-event | ✅ pass |
| 2020-21 chip crunch (demand-side) | — | — | — | demand surge + ordering whiplash while fab capacity stayed intact; shortages persisted ~2 years | ⛔ out-of-scope (no demand dynamics) |

The Sumitomo xfail is deliberate and load-bearing: the model misses that event
twice, in opposite directions (invisible through the fab observable in v0; a
hysteresis trap through delivery in v1), and stays red until the
demand/allocation RFC fixes the model — never by tuning the event
(PLAN.md §3 "honesty artifact", AGENTS.md).

**Pessimism-bias caveat, stated plainly:** the model has no price-mediated
demand allocation, no substitution, no design-around response. It therefore
systematically overstates how deep supply shocks bite and how long they last
— Sumitomo v1 is the standing exhibit. Trust *where fragility concentrates*
(rankings, SPOFs, gradients) more than *how bad it says things get*
(absolute dips and recovery times).

## Reproduce

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt   # jax + pyyaml
python3 -m core.net               # discrete analyses: SPOFs, brittleness, livelock
python3 -m core.gradients         # throughput gradients (capacity + stockpiles)
python3 -m core.adaptation        # adaptation law demo: ALPHA fit + holdouts
python3 -m validation.runner      # all replays; regenerates SCORECARD.md; CI gate
python3 -m fog.priority           # placeholder EVOI ranking of the fog
```

`validation/baseline_outputs.txt` is the frozen regression oracle: those
exact numbers must reproduce (jax 0.10.2, CPU, x64) or you broke something.

## Contributing

Two doors, deliberately asymmetric (details: [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) §2):

- **Data PR** (low friction): improve one number in
  [`map/semiconductors/net.yaml`](map/semiconductors/net.yaml) with a real
  citation. Every parameter is currently `confidence: C` ("session estimate —
  needs citation"). No source, no merge.
- **Subnet PR** (high value): expand an oracle in
  [`map/_oracles/`](map/_oracles/) — match its port places exactly, ship at
  least one historical event, pass the validation gate. `python3 -m
  fog.priority` says which expansion is worth the most (currently:
  `packaging_resin`, opened by the Sumitomo miss).

AI agents: read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before doing anything.
